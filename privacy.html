SILLAGE — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

About SILLAGE

SILLAGE is a personal, non-commercial application for financial tracking, organization and productivity.

Financial information

With the account holder's explicit authorization, SILLAGE may access bank account information, balances and transaction history through Enable Banking.

Purpose of processing

Financial information is used for personal budgeting, account monitoring, transaction categorization and financial analysis.

Authorization and access

Bank account access requires authorization through the relevant Open Banking authentication process. SILLAGE does not request the user's banking password.

Data storage and security

SILLAGE is currently operated locally. Financial data may be stored on the user's device to provide the application's features. Access credentials and financial information are intended to be protected against unauthorized access.

Data sharing

SILLAGE does not sell personal or financial information. Authorized banking providers and financial institutions participate in the account connection process.

Data retention and deletion

Locally stored financial information can be removed by the application owner. Banking connections can be revoked through the available connection controls.

Contact

Privacy inquiries may be sent to the data-protection contact registered with the Open Banking provider.
