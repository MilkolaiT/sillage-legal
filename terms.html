SILLAGE — Terms of Use

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Purpose

SILLAGE is a personal application for financial management, organization and productivity.

Private use

SILLAGE is currently developed for private, non-commercial use and is not offered as a public financial service.

Bank account connections

Bank account information may be accessed through authorized Open Banking providers, subject to the account holder's explicit consent.

Read-only access

The intended banking integration is limited to account information, balances and transactions. SILLAGE does not initiate payments through this feature.

Financial information

Information displayed by SILLAGE is provided for personal informational purposes and does not constitute financial, investment, legal or tax advice.

Availability and accuracy

Banking information may be delayed, incomplete or temporarily unavailable depending on the financial institution and Open Banking provider.

Development status

SILLAGE is under active development. Features may change and technical issues may occur.
